RONA VOICE — THIRD-PARTY NOTICES

This app runs speech recognition and conversation generation on device using Apple frameworks.
Apple Intelligence and English speech assets must be installed before offline use.

VOICE MODEL AND PERSONAS
The supplied YaraVoice_bundle is attributed by its README to Kyutai Pocket TTS
(english, 2026-10). Model weights: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

ONNX graphs derived from KevinAHM/pocket-tts-onnx (Apache-2.0 code; CC BY 4.0 weights).
https://github.com/KevinAHM/pocket-tts-onnx

The supplied persona embeddings are blends of VCTK speakers (CC BY 4.0),
re-voiced using Sesame CSM-1B, according to the supplied bundle README.
https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443
https://github.com/SesameAILabs/csm

The native runtime is a new C++ port of the supplied pocket_onnx.py reference.
Changes: incremental audio decoding, bounded text chunks, cancellation, native model
integration, and audio range validation. Model weights and voice embeddings are unchanged.
Original bundle provenance and checksums are included in YaraVoice_bundle.
